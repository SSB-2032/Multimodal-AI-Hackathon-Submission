# MentorAI — 3-Minute Master Pitch Script (Track D)

## Pitch Breakdown (3 Minutes Total)

### 0:00–0:20 | The Hook
> "Most AI tutors are excellent at answering questions but weak at knowing whether a student actually learned. MentorAI closes that loop."

---

### 0:20–1:05 | Core Workflow & Architecture
> "We ingest textbooks, lecture slides, and video transcripts into a unified, source-cited knowledge base. When a student asks a question, our evidence layer retrieves exact course passages and generates a grounded explanation with inline citations. But we don't stop there. Immediately following the explanation, MentorAI generates an adaptive diagnostic question to verify comprehension."

---

### 1:05–1:30 | The Differentiator (Closed Learning Loop)
> "Our unit of intelligence is not a single query-response pair. It is the loop:
> **Evidence → Explanation → Assessment → Diagnosis → Targeted Intervention → Measured Learning Gain.**
> When a student makes a mistake, our explainable learner model updates skill mastery percentages across granular competencies and instantly triggers a remediation micro-lesson."

---

### 1:30–1:50 | Hard Proof & Learning Gain
> "We don't ask judges to take our word for it—MentorAI includes a built-in evaluation engine that measures diagnostic pre-test vs. post-intervention performance, computing normalized learning gain ($g$). We turn an AI prototype into a validated educational platform."

---

### 1:50–3:00 | Live Resilient Demo & Wrap-Up
1. **Show Source Grounding**: Click "Load Calculus Demo", ask *"What is a removable discontinuity?"*, show exact citation chunks.
2. **Trigger Adaptive Assessment**: Take Question 1 on Continuity, select the wrong answer.
3. **Show Learner State Update**: Point to the "Discontinuity Classification" skill dropping to 40% and the auto-generated targeted follow-up.
4. **Show Learning Gain Dashboard**: Display the $g = 0.67$ normalized gain metric.
5. **Finish with Confidence**: *"MentorAI transforms passive Q&A into active mastery. Thank you."*

---

## Defense Against Fatal Judge Questions

### Q: "Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude?"
> **Answer**: "Generic LLMs provide isolated, uncited answers without tracking skill-level struggles across time. MentorAI maintains a course-grounded evidence index, an explainable learner state model, and an adaptive decision engine that actively guides student progress toward verified mastery."

### Q: "What happens if the API rate limits during live evaluation?"
> **Answer**: "MentorAI is engineered with deterministic local offline fallbacks. Lexical indexing, scoring, skill updates, and diagnostic quizzes run completely offline without external API dependencies."
